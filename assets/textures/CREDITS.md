The realistic look's styling (dark floors, black fabric barriers ~1.1 m high, steel roof with LED strips, black finish gantry)
follows freely licensed race photos on Wikimedia Commons. These are mostly by HybridFitty (CC BY 4.0), plus
"HYROX Istanbul 2026 Pro Men Division" (CC0). The photos themselves are not shipped. HYROX logos and sponsor branding
are deliberately not reproduced.
